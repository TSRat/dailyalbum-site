One album a day,
by chance.

DailyAlbum does not analyze what you liked before. It draws from the classic album lists you choose, leaving room to meet a complete record without a prediction getting there first.

Learn how it worksView ranking sources

A pause from prediction, not a rejection of recommendations.

Recommendation systems are useful in many situations. DailyAlbum simply keeps one part of deliberate classic-album discovery unpredicted: you set the boundaries, chance chooses the next record.

01

What to hear today

DailyAlbum removes heard and ineligible releases from your enabled lists, then makes one stable random draw. An unfinished album carries into tomorrow.

02

What remains afterward

Record completions, relistens, album and track ratings. Timeline, stats, memories, and achievements use facts you explicitly saved.

03

Where music plays

DailyAlbum is not a music player. It hands the exact album to your chosen service and asks before offering an alternative when no exact link is known.

Your records belong to you.

No account is required. Listening records, ratings, timeline, and achievements stay on your device by default, with a full backup you control. DailyAlbum does not read third-party playback history.
