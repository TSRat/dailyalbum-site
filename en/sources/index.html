DailyAlbum editorial work

One-line introductions, Chinese and English wording, three track picks, and broad genres are independently prepared by DailyAlbum. They do not reproduce publication reviews. An album’s title track normally becomes the theme representative; the popular pick excludes intros and interludes; theme and deep-cut choices require editorial judgment.

Artwork, music, and trademarks

Album artwork is used only to identify and describe its release. Music, artwork, ranking names, service names, and trademarks belong to their respective rights holders. DailyAlbum provides no music files and is not an official partner of these publications or Spotify, Apple Music, or NetEase Cloud Music. A source link documents provenance but does not itself grant permission; the public build separately records a rights status for each resource.
