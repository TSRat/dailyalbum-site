DailyAlbum support

Help and support

Protect local records first, then troubleshoot. DailyAlbum has no account sync, so do not begin by deleting the app.

Getting started

Choose a listening service on first launch and decide whether to enable a daily reminder. Today’s album stays stable for the day. If unfinished, it becomes tomorrow’s first album. Finishing one album completes the daily check-in, and you can continue with another.

A listening service will not open

  • Make sure the relevant music app is installed and up to date.
  • Try another service from the album detail.
  • If no exact album is known, DailyAlbum offers a verified alternative or a search. It does not invent an exact link.

Artwork is missing

Check the network connection and reopen the page. A cover-loading failure does not change the daily draw, heard status, ratings, or timeline; the app keeps a local placeholder.

Backup and restore

Open Data and backup at the bottom of Settings and export a complete .dailyalbumbackup file. CSV is for reading and analysis, not restoration. The app creates a local safety backup before a restore.
